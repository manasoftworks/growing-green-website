6. Marketplace and royalties

  • The 4% royalty applies to sales made through the Growing Green marketplace and wholesale channels, capped at $400 per month per farm. Once you reach the cap, every additional sale that month is 0%. The royalty is calculated on the product subtotal only — never on the buyer’s service fee, which also never counts toward the cap.
  • In addition to the royalty, you reimburse Stripe’s processing cost on online sales — currently 2.9% + $0.30 per card charge, calculated on the full amount charged to the buyer. Royalty and processing are collected together as a single Stripe application fee. Example: on a $100.00 marketplace sale the buyer pays $105.00; we collect $4.00 royalty, $3.35 processing and the $5.00 buyer service fee, and $92.65 settles to your Stripe account.
  • In-person point-of-sale transactions carry no royalty — only the payment processor's actual costs, passed through at cost (currently 2.95% + $0.15 per Tap to Pay transaction; 3.15% + $0.30 per typed-card transaction). Cash sales recorded in the app are free.
  • Marketplace and CSA purchases include a buyer-paid service fee of 5% of the product subtotal (minimum $0.50; for CSA shares, capped at $10 per season and applied to the first payment). The fee appears as its own line before you pay — at Market App and CSA checkout and on emailed payment links — and is itemized on every receipt. Wholesale orders and in-person purchases carry no service fee.
  • Farm-offered delivery. You may offer delivery on marketplace orders for a flat fee you set, and you may waive that fee above an order size you choose. The buyer chooses pickup or delivery at checkout and sees the delivery fee before paying. The delivery fee is paid to you, not to Growing Green — the 4% royalty and the buyer’s service fee are calculated on the product subtotal only, never on the delivery fee, and the delivery fee never counts toward the $400 cap. Delivery is arranged and performed by the farm: Growing Green does not deliver any order, does not verify delivery addresses or service areas, and is not a carrier.
  • Marketplace transactions use Stripe Connect in the direct-charges model. You are the merchant of record; Growing Green is a software platform.
  • Marketplace orders are charged when you pick them, not at checkout. The buyer’s payment method is saved at checkout and charged for the quantities actually picked, with the buyer’s service fee recalculated on that final amount. If the final total differs from the buyer’s estimate by more than 15%, or by more than $20.00 — whichever happens first — the buyer is asked to re-confirm before the card is charged.
  • Payouts go directly to the Stripe account you connect during onboarding, weekly, every Friday.

Farms are responsible for holding any licenses or registrations their products and state require for wholesale or retail sale (for example egg licenses, wholesale food-safety registration, or produce-safety filings), and for collecting resale certificates from buyers who purchase for resale, where applicable. Growing Green stores documents farms upload but does not verify any license, certificate, or permit.
